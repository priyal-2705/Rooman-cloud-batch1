About Us

ROOMAN CLOUD was created with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry requirements. We believe students should learn by building real projects instead of only watching videos. Our training focuses on practical implementation, real AWS environments, Docker containers, Jenkins automation, Linux administration and complete CI/CD Pipelines.
